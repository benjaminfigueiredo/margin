\begin{abstract}
Marine heatwaves are becoming more frequent and it is very important to understand how small organisms respond to them.
We exposed copepods to three successive heatwaves and measured survival, egg production and the critical thermal maximum.
Survival declined by 34\% after the third event, while thermal tolerance increased modestly.
\end{abstract}
