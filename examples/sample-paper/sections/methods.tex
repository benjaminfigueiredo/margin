\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

Adult \textit{Acartia tonsa} were collected in May 2025 and maintained at 18\,$^\circ$C.
Each heatwave consisted of a ramp of $1\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\,\mathrm{h}^{-1}$ to 26\,$^\circ$C, held for 72 hours.
Survival was modelled as
\begin{equation}
  \operatorname{logit}(p_i) = \beta_0 + \beta_1 \, \mathrm{event}_i + u_{j[i]},
  \label{eq:survival}
\end{equation}
where $u_j$ is a random effect of replicate tank (see Section~\ref{sec:result}).
